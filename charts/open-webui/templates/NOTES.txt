3. Initial setup

  kubectl wait --namespace {{ .Release.Namespace }} \
    --for=condition=Available deployment/{{ include "open-webui.fullname" . }} \
    --timeout=15m

Create the initial administrator through a private route before exposing a new
installation publicly. First startup can take several minutes while Open WebUI
downloads an embedding model.
