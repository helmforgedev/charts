3. First administrator

   Transmute does not support administrator bootstrap environment variables.
   On a new data volume, the first account created through the application
   becomes the administrator. Protect external access until that account exists.

7. Operations

   Follow application logs:

     kubectl logs --namespace {{ .Release.Namespace }} --follow deployment/{{ include "transmute.fullname" . }}

   Transmute uses SQLite and a ReadWriteOnce data volume. replicaCount must
   remain 1. Recreate prevents two versions from writing the same database
   during an upgrade.
