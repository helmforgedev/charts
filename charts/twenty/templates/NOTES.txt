7. Upgrades and recovery

One Recreate workload serializes migrations and shares storage with its worker.
Native configuration uses environment values; the configuration UI is read-only.
Back up and restore the database, files, matching identity and queues together.

8. Troubleshooting

Inspect the helper and initializer logs before the server and worker.
Confirm the public Service targets proxy port 3000 and never native port 3010.
Readiness cannot substitute for an enforcing CNI during private enrollment.
Existing users require their retained identity marker and encryption key.
Public invite links remain disabled in the chart-managed workspace.
